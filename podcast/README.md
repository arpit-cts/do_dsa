## How every episode note is written

- **The whole conversation as it is in the video**: speaker names, timestamps that jump to the video, and the
  episode's own chapters.
- 🟡 **Yellow** (<mark>like this</mark>): the important lines inside the conversation, plus a **Key points** box
  written separately below each chapter.
- 🔵 **Blue** `Note` boxes: my own research notes, with a verdict and links to the sources.
- **Pictures**: cartoon illustrations (SVG files in each episode's `images/` folder) and Mermaid diagrams.
- A **Try it this week** checklist and a **References** list at the end.
